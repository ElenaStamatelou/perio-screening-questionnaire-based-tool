About this tool

This estimate comes from a logistic regression model built from a research project validating short questionnaires as a periodontitis screening aid. In internal cross-validation on 469 patients from three Dutch cohorts, the model reached an AUC of 0.785 (a measure of how well it ranks people by risk) with calibration close to observed rates (O:E 0.99). That is an estimate of performance on the data it was built from, not a guarantee for you individually.

  • The six questions above are an English adaptation of a questionnaire validated in Dutch (Verhulst et al., 2019). The model's measured accuracy applies to the original Dutch wording — this translation has not been separately validated.
  • The development sample was not a general population: it came from hospital and periodontal-care cohorts, so the mix of people who did and didn't have severe gum disease is not necessarily the same as in the general population.
  • Nothing you type here is transmitted, logged, or stored. The calculation runs entirely in your browser; there is no server behind this page.
  • This is a research prototype, not a certified medical device or clinical decision tool.
